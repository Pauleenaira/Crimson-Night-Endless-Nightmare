# Crimson Night: Endless Nightmare

**Crimson Night: Endless Nightmare** is a web-based endless runner game built with HTML5 Canvas, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript. 

The city is burning, the streets are crawling with shadows, and there is nowhere left to hide. Take control of Yolca and run through the ruins of a city consumed by fire. Leap over burning ground and evade terrifying shadow monsters. Keep running, survive as long as possible, and escape the endless nightmare.

## 🎮 How to Play

Yolca runs forward automatically. Your goal is to jump over obstacles, avoid the shadow monsters, and survive as long as possible. The game speed increases the longer you survive!

**Controls:**
*   **Keyboard:** Press `Spacebar` or `Up Arrow` to jump.
*   **Mouse:** `Click` anywhere on the game canvas to jump.
*   **Touch:** `Tap` the screen on mobile devices.

## ✨ Features

*   **Custom Sprite Animations:** Smooth running cycles and dynamic obstacles.
*   **Progressive Difficulty:** The game speed and obstacle generation scale as your score increases.
*   **Local High Score Tracking:** Your highest survival score is automatically saved in your browser.
*   **Responsive Design:** Playable on desktop browsers, tablets, and mobile phones.
*   **Canvas Fallback System:** Built-in programmatic graphics if image assets fail to load.
